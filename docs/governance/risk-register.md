# Risk register

What could go wrong, how likely and how bad it is, what we do about it, and where that stands. This is the NIST AI RMF *Map* step, and part of the governance set in [ADR 0016](../decisions/0016-governance-and-release-gates.md). Each mitigation points to controls in the [control matrix](control-matrix.md) (C1, C2, ...), and each control points to its code and test.

- **Status:** *draft for owner review* (2026-11-11). The likelihood and impact ratings are judgements, made before any eval ran. Revisit them after the dev baseline, the injection runs and the held-out run, and record what changed.
- **Scale.** *Likelihood*: Low / Medium / High that it happens in normal use of the demo. *Impact*: Low (a mildly wrong or unhelpful answer, noticed) / Medium (a confident wrong answer a user could act on, or a few dollars lost) / High (harm outside the demo: leaked data, real money, a published false claim). *Residual* is the rating after the mitigations that are already implemented.
- **Context.** This is a public Q&A demo over public documents. It has no user accounts, takes no actions, and has a $5 provider credit limit. That context keeps most impacts bounded. It also means the main harms are reputational (a portfolio piece that confidently says something false) and financial (spend).

| R3 | **Direct prompt injection.** A user's question makes the model ignore its rules, reveal its prompt, or produce off-purpose output. | High (anyone can try) | Low | Delimiters and untrusted-data rules (C1, C2); no tools or actions, so an obeyed injection can only change the text (C29); nothing secret in the prompt (C19); 15 direct attacks with canaries, gate at 0 (C4) | Low | Open. | R5 | **Figure misreading.** A value read off a chart or table image is wrong, or the vision description that makes the figure findable is wrong, so the figure is never retrieved. | Medium | Medium | The generator gets the image itself, not just the description (ADR 0008); the judge sees the same image (C11); failed descriptions degrade visibly (C13), coverage gate ≥ 99% (currently 493 of 496); the `--no-figure-images` comparison measures what the image adds | Medium until measured | Open. FAA dev has 0 of its 12 figure questions so far, and held-out has no figure questions planned (owner decision). Until those exist, figure answering is unmeasured. |
| R6 | **Stale or out-of-scope answers.** A user asks about a later filing or handbook revision, or treats an answer as current advice. | Medium | Medium | Questions are answered only from the named fiscal-year filings and the 25C handbook edition ([data card](data-card.md)); citations show which document and page; disclaimer "Not financial, legal, or flight-training advice" (README); AI notice (C15) | Medium | Open. C15 is planned for deployment; the system card should state the snapshot dates. |
| R7 | **Cost abuse (denial of wallet).** Scripted traffic against the live `/ask` drains the provider credit. | Medium once public | Medium | Input and output caps (C23, C24); project-only key with a $5 credit limit (C22); offline runs are capped by the `CostMeter` (C21) | **High once deployed**: the live API has no rate limit or spend cap | Open. Per-IP rate limits and a daily spend cap (C26) and monitoring (C27) are deployment blockers. |
| R8 | **Privacy of live questions.** Users type personal details into a question, which then leaves the machine (to model providers) or sits in logs. | Low | Medium | Zero data retention and no data collection on every model request (C16); key never logged (C17); public-documents-only corpus (C18) | Low for providers; **unknown for logs** | Open. The question-log policy (C20) is a deployment decision: whether to log at all, retention, and no personal data in logs. |
| R9 | **Provider outage or model change.** OpenRouter, a model, or its ZDR endpoint becomes unavailable or changes behaviour; a ZDR endpoint might refuse images. | Medium | Low | Retry on 429, reranker fallback, 502/503 instead of 500 (C28); the `live` smoke test; recorded `finish_reason` (C14) | Low | Open. The judge's ZDR endpoint has not yet been shown to accept images. The figure smoke run (`--type figure`) checks this, and a refusal would need a superseding ADR. |
| R10 | **Eval that doesn't measure what it claims.** Gold pages are wrong or incomplete, questions echo the passage wording, the judge disagrees with a human, or thresholds are tuned after seeing scores. | Medium | High (every quality claim rests on the eval) | Pre-registered, frozen gates (C34); held-out scored once (C35); evidence minimums and one-system rule (C36); calibration of the same judge (C11, C12); review checklist in `evals/README.md`; same-model drafting risk recorded (ADR 0018) | Medium | Open. Depends on the owner's review of all 108 dev drafts and the held-out set. |
| R11 | **Unsafe rendering of model output.** The UI renders an answer as HTML, so text the model was tricked into writing runs in a visitor's browser. | Low | Medium | The API returns typed data, never executes output (C30) | Low in the API; **depends on the UI** | Open. The UI must render answers as plain text (planned in C30). |
| R12 | **Supply chain.** A compromised dependency or a swapped source document. | Low | Medium | Locked dependencies and CI (C32); sources fetched by script from sec.gov and faa.gov with SHA-256 recorded (C33) | Low | Accepted for a demo. Revisit if the corpus takes user uploads. |

## Not in scope

- **Excessive agency (OWASP LLM03).** The model has no tools, no write access and no actions (C29). If tools are ever added, this needs its own entry.
- **Training-data poisoning.** We don't train or fine-tune. Retrieval-time poisoning is R4.
- **High-risk use under the EU AI Act.** A Q&A demo over public documents is not a high-risk use; the Art. 50 transparency duty is covered by C15 (see the [governance README](README.md)).
